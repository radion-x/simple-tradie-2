Privacy

Simple Page Pty Ltd uses the information you provide to respond to website enquiries and manage requested follow-up. Contact hello@simplepage.com.au with privacy questions or requests to access, correct or delete your details.

Enquiries and callback requests

Forms collect your name, phone or email, business details, message and preferred callback window where supplied. Submitted requests are stored in our PostgreSQL lead database and available to authorised staff in a private dashboard. We use Mailgun to send staff notifications and enquiry receipts.

Chat conversations

Questions and recent chat history are sent to OpenRouter and its selected AI provider to generate a reply. Do not enter passwords, payment details or sensitive personal information into the chat. Ordinary chats are held in the page during your visit. If you submit a callback or request a transcript, the conversation is stored with that enquiry and included in the relevant emails. AI providers may process information overseas under their own terms.

Website and staff access

Our hosting and delivery services process technical request information such as IP addresses. Authorised staff access the dashboard using an expiring email link and a secure session cookie.

Optional website analytics

Analytics is off unless you choose to allow it. If you allow it, we use Google Analytics to understand visits, contact-button clicks, form progress and saved enquiries. Google receives technical information about your browser and visit and uses analytics cookies. This helps us see whether the page is useful. A saved enquiry is counted separately from a paying customer.

Our analytics events exclude names, email addresses, phone numbers, form answers, chat conversations and enquiry references. We remove query strings and fragments from page addresses and send only recognised campaign labels. Advertising features and session recording are not enabled. Staff dashboard activity is not measured. Google may process analytics information overseas under its own privacy terms.

Your choice is saved in this browser. You can change it using here or in the website footer. Turning analytics off stops future measurement in this browser. Forms, chat and telephone contact work either way. Previously collected data is not removed by changing this setting; contact us with any questions about your information.

Your choices

You can call or email instead of using a form or chat. We use enquiry information for the requested conversation and business records. Contact us to discuss retention or deletion of your enquiry. We do not add form submissions to a marketing mailing list through this application.
