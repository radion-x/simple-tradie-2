Privacy

Simple Page Pty Ltd uses the information you provide to respond to website enquiries and manage requested follow-up. Contact hello@simplepage.com.au with privacy questions or requests to access, correct or delete your details.

Enquiries and callback requests

Forms collect your name, phone or email, business details, message and preferred callback window where supplied. Submitted requests are stored in our PostgreSQL lead database and available to authorised staff in a private dashboard. We use Mailgun to send staff notifications and enquiry receipts.

Chat conversations

Questions and recent chat history are sent to OpenRouter and its selected AI provider to generate a reply. Do not enter passwords, payment details or sensitive personal information into the chat. Ordinary chats are held in the page during your visit. If you submit a callback or request a transcript, the conversation is stored with that enquiry and included in the relevant emails. AI providers may process information overseas under their own terms.

Website and staff access

Our hosting and delivery services process technical request information such as IP addresses. Authorised staff access the dashboard using an expiring email link and a secure session cookie. If analytics are enabled, additional information about website use may be collected through the configured service. Ask us if you would like details of the current configuration.

Your choices

You can call or email instead of using a form or chat. We use enquiry information for the requested conversation and business records. Contact us to discuss retention or deletion of your enquiry. We do not add form submissions to a marketing mailing list through this application.
